What this means, in plain terms

Raw record — technical view

What this is: live data from Colorado DWR's public REST API (the same system water attorneys and engineers use), pulled through this project's own backend rather than straight from the browser. Search by address or map click uses free U.S. Census geocoding to find the county, then the backend pulls that county's water rights, looks up the parcel at that point, and sorts everything by distance.

What this isn't yet: a guarantee that the nearest listed right on the map actually belongs to, or serves, the property you searched — water rights are tied to a diversion structure (a ditch headgate, well, or reservoir), which is often not on the same parcel as the address you typed. "Nearby" is a starting point for investigation, not proof of ownership. This also isn't a well-yield estimate, a marketplace, or a valuation — those are still ahead.

Sources: dwr.state.co.us/Rest (Colorado DWR) · gis.colorado.gov (parcels) · geocoding.geo.census.gov (geocoding only) · OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
