What this is: live data pulled through this project's own backend from Utah's WRPOD (Water Rights Points of Diversion) dataset — the actual physical location each water right diverts water from, sorted by distance from your search point — Utah's well-log records (location, and for wells with a drilling log on file, real depth/casing/water-level detail), and Utah's statewide parcel dataset (UGRC, all 29 counties in one common schema).

What this isn't yet: place-of-use area matching (whether a right specifically serves this parcel) or a marketplace — those are still ahead, same as Colorado and Idaho.

Sources: Utah Division of Water Rights (WRPOD, well logs) · UGRC statewide parcels · geocoding.geo.census.gov (U.S. Census, geocoding only) · OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
