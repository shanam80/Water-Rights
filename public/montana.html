What this means, in plain terms

What this is: live data from Montana DNRC's Water Right Query System — points of diversion, reservoirs, and place-of-use areas — plus the state's cadastral parcel dataset, pulled through this project's own backend. Search by address or map click looks up the parcel at that point and checks both which rights sit on it and which place-of-use polygons cover it.

What this isn't yet: a guarantee that a nearby right actually serves the property you searched, or a well-yield estimate beyond what's in the state's own well-depth field. This also isn't a marketplace or a valuation.

Sources: gis.dnrc.mt.gov (Montana DNRC — water rights, parcels) · geocoding.geo.census.gov (geocoding only) · OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
