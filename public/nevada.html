What this means, in plain terms

Nearby wells

What this is: live data from Nevada NDWR's water-rights and well-driller-report services, plus a statewide parcel index, pulled through this project's own backend.

What this isn't yet: a guarantee that a nearby right serves the property you searched. Also, Nevada's parcel data here is thinner than other states' — a boundary, acreage, and a link to that county assessor's full record, not owner name/address directly. County codes and permit status codes are shown as Nevada's own raw abbreviations (e.g. "WA", "DEC") rather than translated — only one mapping was ever confirmed live, not enough to safely guess the rest.

Sources: arcgis.water.nv.gov (Nevada NDWR) · geocoding.geo.census.gov (geocoding only) · OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
