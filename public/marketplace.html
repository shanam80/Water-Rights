Water Rights Marketplace

Browse water rights for sale, or list your own. This connects buyers and sellers directly — there's no checkout or payment handling here, since transferring a water right requires the same legal/state review process it always has. Think "contact the seller," not "add to cart."

Fill this out to list a water right. There's no account/login — after submitting, you'll get a private link to manage or remove your listing later. Save that link; it's the only way back in.

Manage your listing

What this is: a listing board connecting water right owners and buyers directly. Submitting a listing requires no account — you get a private management link instead. Contacting a seller (inquiries) is public and doesn't require anything from the buyer beyond a name, email, and message.

What this isn't: a place where money or ownership actually changes hands. Water rights require formal state review to transfer — this is a starting point for that conversation, not a substitute for it. Always confirm a right's actual status directly with the relevant state (linked from each state's lookup page) before relying on anything here.
