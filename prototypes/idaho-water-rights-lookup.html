What this is: a working prototype pulling live from IDWR's public ArcGIS services — the same underlying data as IDWR's own Water Right Locator tool. It checks whether your search point falls inside an actual water right's place-of-use area, across three different stages: confirmed water rights, active permits, and adjudication claims.

What this isn't yet: a well-yield estimate or a marketplace — those are still ahead, same as Colorado. Also, a right's place-of-use shape being at your search point doesn't guarantee it's still active or unmodified — always confirm with IDWR directly before relying on this for a real decision.

Sources: maps.idwr.idaho.gov (IDWR) · geocoding.geo.census.gov (U.S. Census, geocoding only) · OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
